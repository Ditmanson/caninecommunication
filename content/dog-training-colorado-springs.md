I'm a dog trainer serving Colorado Springs and the towns around it: Fountain, Pueblo, Hanover, Woodland Park, Falcon, Ellicott, Yoder, and Calhan. Training starts in your home, where your dog's habits actually happen, and moves out into the city as your dog is ready for it.

## In your home, then out in the world

Your first visit is an [In-Home Consult](/services/in-home-consult/). I meet you and your dog where you live, see what's going on, and build a plan. The $30 deposit is credited toward training, and phone and video consults are free.

Once the foundation is solid, we take it on the road: parks, trailheads, stores, and busy sidewalks, the places your dog needs to listen when it counts. A recall that works in the living room is a start. A recall that works with deer, bikes, and other dogs around is the goal.

## What I help Colorado Springs dog owners with

- **Obedience and recall** through [Food and Play Training](/services/food-and-play-training/)
- **Reliability under distraction and stress** with [Escape and Avoidance Training](/services/escape-and-avoidance-training/)
- **Off-leash freedom** with [E-Collar Training](/services/e-collar-bootcamp/)
- **Barking, lunging, and other bad habits** with [Behavior Modification](/services/behavior-consult/)
- **Reactive dogs** (see the [reactive dogs FAQ](/faq/reactive-dogs/))
- **Kids and the family dog** with [Training with Kids](/services/training-with-kids/)
- **A full program** with the [Fundamentals Bootcamp](/services/fundamentals/)

## Group lessons at Palmer Park

Once your dog is ready, join a [Group Lesson](/services/group-lessons/) at Palmer Park: a pack walk, then nosework, obedience, and ball work.
