Denali is my rescued Husky, born December 14, 2015. She's the one who taught me that force free training has its limits. I used e-collars and light aversive training with her for a couple of years before she became the well-behaved dog she is now.

Today I can trust her with no collar in any environment. Her training is mostly done, so these days we train because she enjoys it, and she shows up in a lot of Griz's sessions.
