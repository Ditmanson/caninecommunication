Denali is my rescued Husky, born December 14, 2015. She's the one who taught me that force free training has its limits. I used negative reinforcement with the e-collar for a couple of years before she became the well-behaved dog she is now.

Today she behaves appropriately in any environment without input from me, and I can trust her with no collar. She still wears the e-collar around prey animals and traffic, because it's not worth the risk to me. Her training is mostly done, so these days we train because she enjoys it, and she shows up in a lot of Griz's sessions.
