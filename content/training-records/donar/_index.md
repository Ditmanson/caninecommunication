Donar is a 5 year old German Shepherd. He came to us with anxiety on car rides that ends in vomiting. His family wants to take him camping and teach him to follow deer trails.

| Play drive | Grabs a toy but doesn't bring it back, enough to build on |

## The plan

Make the vehicle a place Donar wants to be, then build the skills his family wants on top of it.

- **Food first:** use [food](/services/food-and-play-training/) to teach article indication and entry level tracking.
- **Chill in the vehicle:** set a schedule for Donar to [settle](/services/food-and-play-training/#settling) in the vehicle while it's off.
- **A safe space:** use positive reinforcement to make the vehicle a place he wants to be.
- **Stress training:** start [force fetch and leash pressure](/services/escape-and-avoidance-training/).

## Granny's rule

The [Premack principle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premack%27s_principle), aka Granny's rule: each activity rewards the one before it. We use it to make him love the vehicle.

| First | Then | Then |
| -- | -- | -- |
| Stress training | Place in the vehicle | Play |
| Stress training | Place in the vehicle | Article indication, entry level tracking |
