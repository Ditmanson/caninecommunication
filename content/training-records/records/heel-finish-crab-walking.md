A friend asked about crab walking in the heel. It's a hard issue in competition heeling. It's a classically conditioned response to be closer to the reward. Look up reward placement videos for more, but essentially I fix it by changing where the reward is. Here I let it fall way behind us, so if she wants to get closer to the reward, she has to fall back.
