Once a day I do this with my dogs in some fashion. In this video I'm listening to an audiobook in my shed. Sometimes I do it in the house with the family while watching a movie. Maybe I have company over, we're hanging out by a fire, and my dogs are acting a fool, so I put them in a down and work chill training.

In this video I'm shaping focus too. That's not necessary, but if you're doing chill training with just you and your pups, you might as well practice.

To work on focus, I mark eye contact before rewarding. I'm asking for about 10 seconds of sustained eye contact here, but it started with just an instant. If this video had sound, you wouldn't hear a verbal marker at all. I mark eye contact by raising my eyebrows. It might be silly, but it beats a verbal marker: with a verbal marker you might miss the eye contact and mark your dog looking away. With an eyebrow raise, if you mark too late, it brings their eyes back to your face.
