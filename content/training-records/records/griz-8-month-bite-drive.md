I'm going through Haz's videos from Shield K9 online. He has some on building drive, which he calls activating the dog. The idea is pretty simple:

1. Chase, reward
2. Frustrate, chase, reward
3. Frustrate, obedience, chase, reward

I'm using that idea with a back tie to get faster sits and downs out of Griz. My frustration: her sit on a `here` or `front` command is great, but her sit at any distance is slow and rolled back, and her downs are the same. It's crazy, because her sit in motion looks better than a sit at 5 feet out. That's what we're working on.
