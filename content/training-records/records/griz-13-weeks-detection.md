Look how tiny she is! This is something I used to do with my old dog. The scent we pick here doesn't matter. If you have something specific to work on, like narcotics, then by all means train with that. I've found it's pretty easy to introduce new smells later. My last dog could be turned onto a new scent with 2-3 easy reps.

For IGP we're going to need her to use her nose in a different way, but this is an easy way to work the nose every day.

The fundamental skill here is shaping. I think that's why I like nosework so much: you get to watch the dog teach herself. It's cool.
