The goal is to give power to her barks. I want her to think she can make me come to her, or make me flee, with her barks. If I ask her to get scary, we want to see powerful barks.

To get this we use shaping and frustration with the tug. As she barks, I move closer. At first I reward any vocal bark with forward motion and avoid resetting. Over time I want stronger, more sustained barks. Eventually this turns into a game where I can move back or go still, and she brings me to life with barking.
