About a week after [Bark Training 1](/training-records/records/bark-training-1/). We've done the tie back a few times, and I think I can get her to do it with me holding her back. This can be confusing for her, and I wouldn't try it if I didn't think she was ready. We'll do this a few more times before adding a helper.

The technique is shaping again, but this time instead of me getting closer to her, we get closer to the ball. I see some guys on YouTube using agitation collars for this, but I want to keep my leash pressure communication strong, so I use a harness most of the time.
