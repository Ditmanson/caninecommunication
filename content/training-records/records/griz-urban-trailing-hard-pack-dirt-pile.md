## Urban search and rescue trailing

An urban search and rescue training. The trail is about 40 minutes old. I was given a point last seen and a direction of travel, and I knew there was 1 unmarked corner to work through. There's a distraction with another dog along the way. The goal was to help me learn when Griz is on odor and when she's following drifting odor.

The first video is portrait, the way it was filmed. The second is the same video cropped to landscape for YouTube.

## Hard pack tracking

Same day. I noticed she struggles on hard pack. When she loses a track, we'd hope she starts trailing to stay on odor, but I can also improve her tracking through hard pack, low-odor areas. That's what we're doing here.

While walking the dogs I saw this dirt pile and wanted to see how Griz would track over it in a straight line. I'm not sure it helps with anything other than a new experience, but it's kind of cool. Maybe I'll do it again and see if she improves.
