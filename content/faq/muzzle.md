---
title: "Why muzzle train a dog that doesn't bite?"
weight: 73
outputs: ["html", "fragment"]
---

Muzzle training is good for dogs even if they aren't a danger to anyone. It's an activity we can use to teach your dog how to deal with stress, and it gives you another tool you can use right now while we get past whatever your dog needs to get past. For dogs with a [bite history](/faq/bite-history/), it comes first.

For some small breeds, like pugs, a muzzle is overkill. In those cases an Elizabethan collar (the cone from the vet) works: it gives us the same controlled practice on gear you're more likely to use.
