title: "Do you do protection or bite work?"
weight: 66
outputs: ["html", "fragment"]
---

No. Protection and bite work is a complex task. To do it properly, a dog has to be put into defensive drive to see how they react, and that takes a team and a decoy. These aren't skills I've practiced, and they need a bigger team than I have.

We can teach bite work in play drive, but that's not the same thing. It should be treated as play, not confused with real protection training.
