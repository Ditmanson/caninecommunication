Flat collars are the most common collar. They're where the tags go, and they look nice, but they're not for leash pressure: all the pressure lands on the front of the throat, so use a [prong collar](/faq/prong-collar/) for that. They're not exactly useless, though. I use one for footstep tracking, with the long lead between her legs so pulling just brings her nose closer to the ground. I never make aversive corrections on the track. I also use flat collars and fur savers to classically condition a dog to a job: this collar on means we're doing air scent training. And a flat collar can hold her in place if I tie her up outside a store.
