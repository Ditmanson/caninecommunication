title: "What is a flat collar good for?"
weight: 65
outputs: ["html", "fragment"]
---

Flat collars are the most common collar and the least useful for training. They look nice, but a correction puts all the pressure on the throat.

I still use one on occasion, mostly for tracking. I run the long lead between her legs, so the pressure lands on the back of the neck instead of the throat. I never make aversive corrections on the track, but if she pulls into the lead this way, it turns her head in the direction of the scent.
