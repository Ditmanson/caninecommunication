## Family

Yes, I'd love to have the whole family there. Consistency is key, and when everyone is on board, results show up faster.

## Multiple dogs

I train dogs one at a time at first. We start with as few distractions as possible while we build the foundation. Once we know what your dog knows and they're ready for a busier environment, other dogs can join.

Each session focuses on one dog, and billing is per session, not per dog. Some sessions are about the dogs together, like walking or playing with both when that's the problem. That's still one session.
