---
title: "Do I need to be there, or do you train my dog for me?"
weight: 16
outputs: ["html", "fragment"]
---

For the most part, you need to be there. The goal is a better life for you and your dog, and that comes from learning to communicate clearly what you want and expect.

Some lessons can be done without you. For example, I can work through desensitizing your dog's fear of a specific trigger on my own. You'll still meet with me enough times to understand what I'm doing. I won't take your dog for training unless you know exactly what I'm doing and why it works.
