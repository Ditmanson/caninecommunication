For nosework, a harness is mostly interchangeable with an [agitation collar](/faq/agitation-collar/). The benefit is the classically conditioned response: I use the harness for trailing and the collar for tracking.

When Griz gets the harness on, she knows she can go fast and nobody cares about every footstep. When she gets the collar on, she knows there are small articles and food on the track, so she slows down.
