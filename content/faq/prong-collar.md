title: "What is a prong collar good for?"
weight: 68
outputs: ["html", "fragment"]
---

A prong (pinch) collar gives clear, adjustable pressure. It can be used for momentary or constant pressure, and corrections can start very light and escalate only as needed. It gets a bad rap because it can be used for positive punishment, which is sometimes necessary, but that's not its main job.

**Sizing:** better too tight than too loose. The collar should rotate on the dog, but make full contact all the way around the neck when the martingale is pulled tight. Too loose and a correction hits a smaller surface area, which can injure your dog. Two dogs in loose prongs can also get their collars tangled in a fight.
