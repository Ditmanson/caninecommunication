title: "What is a slip collar good for?"
weight: 67
outputs: ["html", "fragment"]
---

A slip collar is great for continuous pressure, which makes it good for negative reinforcement ([escape and avoidance](/faq/escape-and-avoidance/)). It doesn't give the snappy corrections of a pinch collar, but it looks nicer.

I use one my wife made all the time. It doubles as an 18" slip lead, and it's Griz's primary collar, so it's the one with her tags.
