---
title: "What is a slip collar good for?"
weight: 67
outputs: ["html", "fragment"]
---

A slip collar is useful for the same reasons as a [prong collar](/faq/prong-collar/): it teaches negative reinforcement ([escape and avoidance](/faq/escape-and-avoidance/)) with a leash. It's convenient, and people like the look, since you see them at the vet's office all the time. But it takes more pressure than a prong to get the same effect, and it tightens in a way that's more dangerous to the throat. I'd always recommend a prong over it, but it's convenient once your dog is confident with leash pressure.

I use one my wife made all the time. It doubles as an 18" slip lead, and it's Griz's primary collar, so it's the one with her tags.
