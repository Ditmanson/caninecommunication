Every training plan is built around your goals. When it ends, you continue your new life with your dog, and I'm still here to help:

- **Questions:** call, text, or email me any time.
- **Discord community:** clients get an invite to my Discord to ask questions, share progress, and connect with other handlers. I'll send you the link.
- **[Group Lessons](/services/group-lessons/):** keep practicing around other dogs.
- **Follow-ups:** book more sessions whenever you want them.

Many people cherish their relationship with their dog and keep finding ways to deepen the bond, whether in everyday life or through new skills.
