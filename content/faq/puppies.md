Right away, but it isn't fair to train a puppy the same way as an adult dog.

When you bring a puppy home at 8 weeks, their eyes haven't fully developed and the world is big and scary. Around 4–8 months, puppies go through a fear period where the world seems scarier than before. We navigate both carefully.

## Aversives and puppies

We can and should start teaching leash pressure early, but puppies don't need the extra pressure of a pinch collar. Instead, we work with their opposition reflex (the instinct to pull against pressure) and reward with food or play.

Your puppy can start wearing a pinch collar or e-collar so they get used to it, but we likely won't use them. A puppy's understanding of the world is low, and so are the likely consequences of their behavior. We don't need aversive training until your dog understands the world around them and their actions could hurt themselves or others.
