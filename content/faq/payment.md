## Payment

I accept cash, check, Venmo, or Zelle, paid at the consult and at each session. See [Pricing](/faq/pricing/) for rates.

## Late cancellations

Life happens, and I understand that. If something important comes up and you need to cancel or reschedule late, that's okay. Please give at least 24 hours' notice when you can (see [booking and rescheduling](/faq/booking-rescheduling/)). If late cancellations become a habit, our working relationship will come to an end.
