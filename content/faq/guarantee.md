Results depend on the techniques we use, your dog's history, how much time we put into each skill, and which cornerstones of training we use to get there. So I can't promise a specific outcome by a specific date.

What I do guarantee: you'll see results, and you'll leave with a clear understanding of how to keep moving toward better ones.

## If you're not satisfied

If a session doesn't meet your expectations, tell me and I'll refund it, minus $30 to cover travel. Then we'll talk about what isn't working. If we can't get aligned, I'll help you find a better fit.

Equipment I buy for you can be sold back to me at 90% of its value. See [Pricing](/faq/pricing/#equipment-buyback).
