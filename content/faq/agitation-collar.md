---
title: "What is an agitation collar good for?"
weight: 69
outputs: ["html", "fragment"]
---

An agitation collar is built to be pulled on, hard. It looks like a big flat collar, but the wide, padded surface keeps the dog from getting choked and hurting her throat. It's the best collar for building drive: it creates frustration, and frustration builds drive. There's no collar better for building play drive on a dog who's already peaking with conventional play. You see them a lot in protection clubs. I don't train protection, but I use it the same way. They're a ton of fun and make your dog look cool, but people use them like normal collars. For walking and leash pressure, use a [prong collar](/faq/prong-collar/). For tracking it's interchangeable with a [harness](/faq/harness/).
