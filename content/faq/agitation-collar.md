---
title: "What is an agitation collar good for?"
weight: 69
outputs: ["html", "fragment"]
---

An agitation collar is built for the dog to pull against. It looks like a big flat collar, but it spreads pressure across a much wider area and is padded for the dog to push into, so the risk to the throat is close to zero.

You see them a lot in protection clubs, where they're used to bring a dog's drive up. I use it the same way, and not just for protection: it builds drive for any task. For tracking it's interchangeable with a [harness](/faq/harness/).
