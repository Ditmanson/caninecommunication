Two lengths:

- **6"–3' leash:** light enough that your dog can wear it with a [prong collar](/faq/prong-collar/) without accidental discomfort.
- **8'–30' leash:** long enough for recalls and freedom on enrichment walks. I also use a long lead for tracking with a [flat collar](/faq/flat-collar/).
