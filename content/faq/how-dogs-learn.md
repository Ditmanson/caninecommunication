Dogs don't understand English. They learn that certain words predict what happens next. "Good" means something only if it predicts praise or play; "no," only if it predicts leash pressure or losing a toy. Teaching this isn't intuitive, and small mistakes can teach a word to predict an outcome we never intended.

## Classical conditioning

Half of this communication system pairs words and sounds with what we do next. This is [classical conditioning](https://www.khanacademy.org/test-prep/mcat/behavior/learning-slug/v/classical-conditioning-neutral-conditioned-and-unconditioned-stimuli-and-responses): it creates an involuntary response to a once-neutral stimulus, which is how verbal markers control emotional responses.

## Operant conditioning

Dogs can't speak English to us either. They learn that their own actions have predictable outcomes: hanging out in the kitchen at dinner means snacks, and coming when called means play. They also learn that cats run when chased and that barking changes their surroundings, so they chase and bark more.

The other half of the system gives behaviors clear consequences, to encourage the ones we want and extinguish the ones we don't. This is [operant conditioning](https://www.khanacademy.org/test-prep/mcat/behavior/learning-slug/v/operant-conditioning-positive-and-negative-reinforcement-and-punishment): it shapes behavior through consequences.

## Every training style uses both

All dog training — force free, Traditional (also called escape and avoidance training), balanced, or accidental — traces back to these two kinds of conditioning. Master both, and you can create or extinguish any behavior your dog is (or isn't) offering.

Every bad habit I extinguish and every good habit I build lives in the four quadrants of operant conditioning. This isn't just dog training; it's psychology, laid out by B.F. Skinner in [*The Behavior of Organisms*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Behavior_of_Organisms) (1938). Decades of sports and marine wildlife training have refined techniques for creating and extinguishing behaviors efficiently, and my communication system puts them to work.
