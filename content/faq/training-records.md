weight: 80

A training record is part of a [Capstone](/services/capstone/), usually after many sessions. We record a video of your dog's skills, and I write notes on what's going well and what to improve.

You get a copy of your video and notes. With your permission, I host them on the [Training Records](/training-records/) page. You can opt out and have it removed at any time.
