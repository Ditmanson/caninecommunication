## Sessions

Training sessions are **$100 each**. Because every plan is built around your dog, the total depends on which services you need. For example, [Verbal Markers](/services/verbal-markers/) takes one or two sessions, and a full [Fundamentals](/services/fundamentals/) build is about seven.

[Group Lessons](/services/group-lessons/) are $30 per dog. [E-Collar Training](/services/e-collar-bootcamp/) is billed per session, plus the cost of the collar.

## The consult deposit

Every client starts with an [In-Home Consult](/services/in-home-consult/), booked with a **$30 deposit**. The deposit:

- is credited toward your first training session if you book within 60 days
- is non-refundable
- is waived if I decide at the consult that I can't help your dog

## Equipment

At the consult, we'll go over what you have and what you need. Some gear you own may be the wrong size.

- **Treat pouch:** I used to use pockets, but a dedicated pouch is a game changer.
- **6"–3' leash:** Light enough that your dog can wear it with a pinch collar without accidental discomfort.
- **8'–30' leash:** Long enough for recalls and freedom on enrichment walks.
- **Herm Sprenger [pinch collar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dog_collar#Aversive_collars):** It looks mean, but it's a game changer. It's less likely to injure your dog than a flat collar (I'll explain at the consult), and it delivers low-intensity aversive stimulation safely while still extinguishing behaviors.
- **Harness or agitation collar:** Something your dog can pull against to build frustration without hurting their throat. Some dogs can get by with a flat collar.
- **[Muzzle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muzzle_(mouth_guard)):** I require muzzle training even if your dog isn't dangerous. It gives us a controlled way to practice habituation with rewards or corrections. It also means that in [Group Lessons](/services/group-lessons/), every dog that needs a muzzle is wearing one.
    - For some small breeds, like pugs, a muzzle is overkill. In those cases an [Elizabethan collar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabethan_collar) works: it gives us the same controlled practice on gear you're more likely to use.

## Equipment buyback

When training ends, I'll buy back any equipment you don't want at 90% of its value. That gear goes to future clients who need to save money. Do the math: if I buy back the same piece of gear 10 times, some lucky handler gets it free.
