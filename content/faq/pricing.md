## Sessions

Training sessions are **$100 each**. Sessions are designed to run about an hour, sometimes a little longer or shorter. You're charged by the session, not by the hour. Because every plan is built around your dog, the total depends on which services you need. For example, [Food and Play Training](/services/food-and-play-training/) takes one or two sessions, and a full [Fundamentals](/services/fundamentals/) build is about seven.

[Group Lessons](/services/group-lessons/) are $30 per dog. [E-Collar Training](/services/e-collar-bootcamp/) is billed per session, plus the cost of the collar.

## The consult deposit

Every client starts with an [In-Home Consult](/services/in-home-consult/), booked with a **$30 deposit**. The In-Home Consult runs about 30 minutes. Optional video or phone consults beforehand are free. The deposit:

- is paid at the consult
- is credited toward your first training session if you book within 60 days
- is non-refundable
- is waived if I decide at the consult that I can't help your dog

## Equipment buyback

None of my [recommended gear](/faq/collars/#what-each-piece-of-gear-is-for) is required. Equipment I buy for you can be sold back to me at 90% of its value when training ends. That gear goes to future clients who need to save money. Do the math: if I buy back the same piece of gear 10 times, some lucky handler gets it free.

## Payment

Cash, check, Venmo, or Zelle, paid at the consult and at each session. See [How do I pay, and what if I cancel late?](/faq/payment/)
