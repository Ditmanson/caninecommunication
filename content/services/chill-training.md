summary: "Teach your dog to settle, even when excited, by adding distance and duration to their commands."
---

The goal is to "flatten" your dog: lower their excitement, and teach them to settle even when aroused. We add distance and duration to the commands from [Verbal Commands](/services/verbal-commands/).

Discussion is light and centers on [reward schedules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reinforcement#Intermittent_reinforcement_schedules). We'll cover how to fade your help, whether that's the leash or luring, and how to fade the place box. It's useful, but you won't always have one in the real world.
