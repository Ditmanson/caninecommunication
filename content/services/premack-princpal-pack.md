# What is the Premack Principal
[Wikipedia Link](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premack%27s_principle)

We can ignore all that though. Premack's principle is basically an extension on classical conditioning; except instead of an association between two stimuli, we create an association between two behaviors. What we are doing is increasing the drive for each activity by associating it with another.
We use this as a giant positive reinforcement session. We start with a pack walk. The pack walk might be stressful for some dogs; however, we give them something to look forward to. This will help release positive hormones such as dopamine to help battle all the cortisol flooding the system. This helps with desensitization to habituation of the activity. After the pack walk we'll get together to do some nosework training. Nosework is naturally a secondary reinforcer, meaning after dogs learn the game, it will be self rewarding. After that we'll hit obedience training, this can always improve. We'll find that the use of corrections and negative reinforcement won't be as aversive here as it has been in other places. The reason for that is we have a giant reward event coming next. At the end we'll continue to improve skills with the dog; except we'll be rewarding with the ball or disc. Eventually, I have some games to teach that can be either played with just a handler and their dog, or we can keep score and compete with each other.
