# Why the E-collar
We want to maintain the ability to provide pressure and/or corrections to our dog, but we don't want to carry a leash. 

# 2 Methods
There's 2 different philosophies we can embrace here, method 1 is where we have a collar dumb dog, _meaning the dog doesn't know the collar is providing the corrections_ and method 2 where we have a collar savoy dog. We can always transition to from method 1 to method 2, but we can not transition from method 2 to method 1.
## Method 1
In method 1, we use the collar sparingly and the conquense is we can solve some problems quickly and permenatly and be able to remove the collar earlier in our dogs development. In this method the e-collar is strictly used for positive punishment.

We go out of our way to prevent the dog from learning what the collar or remote is. We use it for things like jumping up on people, counter surfing, digging, chewing on stuff, and other negative behaviors. We can try using it for recall; however, using it for recall significantly increases our chances of getting a collar savvy dog. In this method we use the collar as little as possible at high levels of stimulation

## Method 2
The e-collars usefullness expands greatly if we are willing to have a collar savvy dog. If we are going to let the dog understand the purpose of the collar, then we can use it for negative reinforcement as well as positive punishment. We can incorporate it into any of our training, and this in this method is where we find ourseleves with some extra sessions. We will use it with negative reinforcement for recall, heeling, barking, reactivity. 

Because our goals have shifted from keeping our dog collar dumb to using the collar as a primary rule enforcer, we get to switch from a high stim to a low stim. In this method we keep our collars turned down to 10-25 depending on the dog. I greatly favor this method, but it is important to be consious that we can never turn a collar savvy dog into a collar dumb dog.
