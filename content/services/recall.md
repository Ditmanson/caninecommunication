#### "Recall is the most important skill a dog needs. Why wait until the end?"

We haven't. Every earlier session built engagement through distractions to make this part easy and fun. This session isn't where we teach recall; it's where we bulletproof it.

We play a variety of recall games with lots of [positive reinforcement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reinforcement#Positive_reinforcement). But we also need an environment stimulating enough to call for some negative reinforcement or correction. Neither of us wants your dog to ignore a recall in a dangerous situation. The aversives I use are controlled and won't harm your dog. The aversives the world uses to enforce a recall can be fatal, so we do this right.
