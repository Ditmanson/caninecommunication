## Overview

Together we identify the cause of your dog's reactivity and agree on a plan. The plan depends on the cause: fear-based reactivity needs very different methods than predatory reactivity.

## Fear-based reactivity

For fear, we work on [desensitization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systematic_desensitization) and [counterconditioning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counterconditioning). We bring your dog closer to their trigger and use classical conditioning to change the association from danger to fun, food, or at least no fear, until they reach [habituation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habituation).

## Predatory reactivity

Predatory aggression can only be solved with Traditional training, also called escape and avoidance training ([training styles](/faq/training-styles/)). We will:

- [Muzzle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muzzle_(mouth_guard)) train your dog
- Condition a **no** marker
- Condition [leash pressure](/faq/escape-and-avoidance/)
- Hold your dog accountable with predictable consequences for their choices
