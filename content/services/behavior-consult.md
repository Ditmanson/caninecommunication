# Why do I need fundamentals boot camp to hire you?

There are many cases where a handler goes to a trainer to fix a problem, the trainer fixes the problem, the handler brings their dog home, and the problem reemerges. This happens when handlers are the source of the problem. I find that is usually the case. Through means of classical and/or operant conditioning applied accidetnly, owners are often unintentionally training their dogs to be reactive. Start with me in the fundamentals boot camp and we can rule this out. 

# What

By now you and I have a bit of a relationship. You know my methods standard training procedures. The plan you and I make together should make perfect sense. Together we'll agree on the causation of the dogs reactivity, and I'll come up with a plan to start desensitizing.

# How
I'll pick your dog up, bring them into an environment that's as close to the triggers as I think I can get without crossing the dogs threshold. I'll train the dog just inside their threshold. For every time we get close to the threshold without unwanted behaviors, I'll reward. If we cross the threshold and reactivity shows, I'll use leash corrections to bring the dog back under control and shape the behavior with positive punishment. 

# Why
Operant Conditioning. When the dog behaves correctly closer and closer to their triggers, we reward, reinforcing the behavior. When the dog crosses the threshold, we punish the behavior, reducing the intensity and frequency at which it will occur again. 

When possible, I prefer to deal with these situations in a force free manner. Usually the dog is just afraid and needs to learn through habituation that nothing bad is going to happen if they are with you or me. I want the dogs to look to us for safety and not feel the need to defend themselves or us.
