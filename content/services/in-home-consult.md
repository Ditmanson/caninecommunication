## What happens at the consult

I meet you and your dog where you live. We talk through what's going on, what you want out of training, and which services fit. Most dogs start with [Verbal Markers](/services/verbal-markers/), the foundation everything else builds on.

For dangerously reactive dogs, we'll discuss how many desensitization sessions are needed before training can start. I need to be able to work safely around you and your dog, so an overly reactive dog may need [Behavior Modification](/services/behavior-consult/) first.

## The deposit

The consult is a $30 deposit, credited toward your first training session if you book within 60 days. It's non-refundable, unless I decide at the consult that I can't help your dog, in which case it's waived. See [Pricing](/faq/pricing/) for details.
