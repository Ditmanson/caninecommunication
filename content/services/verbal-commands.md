## Higher-order conditioning

Commands are verbal markers. We call them [higher-order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second-order_conditioning) markers because they get their meaning from the reward and correction markers your dog already knows. *Down* is a command, but it's also a continuation marker: if your dog stays in the down, they either earn an occasional reward or stay to **avoid** pressure ([negative reinforcement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reinforcement#Negative_reinforcement)).

We focus on the commands every handler needs:

- Sit
- Down
- Place
- Here
- Others as needed. We add or drop commands to fit your goals.

## Engagement

We also work on engagement between you and your dog, and on ignoring distractions. At first the distraction is probably just me. If your dog is ready, we'll move somewhere more stimulating and let the environment do the distracting.
