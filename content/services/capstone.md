A capstone is whatever you and I decide your dog should show off. It might wrap up a full [Fundamentals](/services/fundamentals/) build, or be a single goal you care about. We design it together at the level your dog is ready for: as stimulating an environment and as fast and precise as they can handle. The better you did with your homework, the better it can be.

## Show both sides

A good capstone shows the layering. [Food and Play Training](/services/food-and-play-training/) gives your dog drive, speed, and precision. [Escape and Avoidance Training](/services/escape-and-avoidance-training/) makes it hold up under stress. We usually show a behavior with excitement first, then show it holding in a stimulating environment.

## What we can show

- **Enrichment walking:** *How do we handle [leash pulling](/services/escape-and-avoidance-training/#leash-pulling-and-heeling)?*
- **Impulse control:** *How does your dog go through a doorway, or wait for permission to eat?*
- **Settling:** *How long will your dog hold a place, down, or sit without rewards or corrections? If they still need them, what does the [reward schedule](/services/food-and-play-training/#settling) look like, and how often do we [reapply pressure](/services/escape-and-avoidance-training/#settling)?*
- **Heeling:** *How good is the [heel](/services/food-and-play-training/#heeling)? Can it hold your dog's attention in heavy distraction, or keep them safe in unsafe conditions?*
- **Recall:** *SPEED! SPEED! SPEED! How fast does your dog come, and how strong can the distractions get while [recall](/services/escape-and-avoidance-training/#recall) stays bulletproof?*

## Training records over certifications

I'm not planning to certify for the [AKC Canine Good Citizen](https://www.akc.org/products-services/training-programs/canine-good-citizen/) or [IGP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schutzhund) BH. I believe in training records over certifications. A certification proves a fixed standard; the Canine Good Citizen is a good one, but it doesn't meet your dog where they are and may be too hard or too easy right now. A training record shows exactly where you and your dog are.

## What we record

We make a video, and I write notes on what's going well and what to improve. You keep the video for your own records, or I host it on the site. See [Training Records](/training-records/) for examples.
