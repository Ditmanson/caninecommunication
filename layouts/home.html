How I train

Every day you see well-trained, well-behaved dogs out with their humans, and you’ve wondered: why doesn’t my dog look like that? You turned to the internet and ChatGPT for help, and so far it hasn’t produced the results you’re looking for. Now you’re here, wondering if I can help.

Dog training is mostly simple. However, the way we communicate with our dogs isn’t always as intuitive as it looks when you compare yourself to others. I invite you to sit down and talk with me, and together we can solve the challenges that have made life with your dog harder than it needs to be.

I’ll show you modern techniques from all four corners of dog training. We’ll learn food and play training, also known as force free dog training, and pair it with modern escape and avoidance training, also known as Traditional dog training, to create a clear channel of communication between you and your dog. At each step, you’ll be 100% confident in what your dog knows and how to apply what you learn with me to any situation, in any environment.

The most important thing to understand about our dogs is that our verbal language is unintuitive to them. They don’t learn English the way we do. To give them a vocabulary, we start slow and build associations with the important words, sounds, and phrases we want them to react to. This is called classical conditioning. Once we’re sure that foundation is clear, we use it to show the dog which behaviors we expect. This is operant conditioning: learning through consequences.

For each behavior, you’ll learn how to use the cornerstones of modern dog training to shape it however you see fit. In each session, you’ll understand which cornerstones are available for that behavior and the drawbacks of relying on only one, and you’ll have complete confidence in the result we can expect.
