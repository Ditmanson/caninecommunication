A Complete Training System.

All dog training — force free, traditional, balanced, or accidental — traces back to classical or operant conditioning. Through the bootcamp you’ll master both, and be able to create or extinguish any behavior your dog is (or isn’t) offering.

Classical conditioning

Creates an involuntary response to a neutral stimulus — the control of emotional responses through verbal markers.

Operant conditioning

Shapes behavior through consequences.
Book a Session

Every client starts in the bootcamp

You’ll learn both kinds of conditioning in three training styles. After that, the rest of K9-Communication’s services open up to you.

Force Free

Training without aversives.

Traditional

Training with a focus on aversives.

Balanced

A blend of force free and traditional training.

The Fundamentals Bootcamp

Session 1

Reward Markers

We start with classical conditioning and reward markers, plus impulse control.

Session 2

Leash Pressure

More operant conditioning, and learning to communicate through the leash. Traditional and balanced training.

Session 3

Higher Order Conditioning

We combine the first two lessons and start building commands through higher order conditioning. All styles shown, with focus on the one you prefer.

Session 4

Chill Training

Teach your dog to chill out, stay put, and hold a down or sit for extended periods. We encourage with force free methods and enforce with traditional ones, bulletproofing the behavior.

Session 5

Heeling

Heeling is dynamic and can be frustrating until you understand it. Together we’ll make it fun with force free methods, or precise and bulletproof with traditional ones.

Session 6

Recall Training

The most fun and most important lesson. We teach both force free and traditional methods, and strongly encourage both. After so much purposeful engagement, you’ll be surprised how easy it is.

Session 7

Capstone

We video record a capstone showing everything you’ve learned. Your training record can be shared publicly here, provided privately to you, or both — whatever you prefer.
