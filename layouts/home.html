How I train

Dogs don’t understand English. They learn what our words predict (classical conditioning) and what their actions earn (operant conditioning). All dog training — force free, Traditional (also called escape and avoidance training), balanced, or accidental — traces back to these two. Master both, and you can create or extinguish any behavior.

Force free
Training without aversives.
Traditional
Training with a focus on aversives.
Balanced
A blend of force free and traditional training.
