A Complete Training System.

All dog training; whether it's force free, traditional, balanced, or accidental; can be traced back to classical or operant conditioning. Classical conditioning creates an involuntary response to a neutral stimulus. This is the control of emotional responses to verbal verbal markers. Operant Conditioning on the other hand, is shaping behavior through consequenses. Through the boot camp you'll master these skills and be able to create or extinguish any behaviors your dog is or is not creating.

Every client starts in a boot camp where we learn classical and operant conditioning in the styles of: - Force Free _training without aversives_ - Traditional Training _training with a focus on aversives_ - Balanced Training _A blend of force free and traiditional training_ After which the rest of K9-Communication's services become available to you

The Fundamentals Bootcamp

Reward Markers

We start by learning about classic conditioning and reward markers. We will also learn about impulse control.

Leash Pressure

Now we will spend more time on operant conditioning and learning how to communicate through the leash, _this lesson is traditional and balanced training_

Higher Order Conditioning

Now we put our first two lessons together and start creating commands through higher order conditioning _All training styles shown on this day with focus on the style you prefer._

Chill Training

On this day we learn how to get our dogs to chill out, stay put, down and sit for extended periods of time. _We encourage through force free methods, and enforce with traditional methods, giving us a balanced training style that bullet proofs our chill out commands_

Heeling

Heeling is extremely dynamic. It can be very frustrating when you don't understand what your doing, but together we will learn how to make it fun with force free methods, or how to make it percise and bullet proof with traditional styles

Recall Training

The funnest and most important lesson of the boot camp. How do we bulletproof recalls? We teach both force free and traditional methods and strongly encourage both. By this point in time we will have spent so much time with purposeful engagement, you will be surprised by how easy it is.

Capstone

We video record a capstone to show everything you have learned. Your training records are either shared here and available to the public, or provided to you for your own record keeping, or both. Whatever you prefer.
